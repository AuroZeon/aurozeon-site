ByeBites Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Introduction

Welcome to ByeBites. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the application.

Information We Collect

We may collect several types of information from and about users of our application, including:

  • Personal information such as height, weight, and food preferences that you voluntarily provide when using our application.
  • Information about your device, including your mobile device ID, IP address, and operating system.
  • Usage data such as the features you use, the time spent on the application, and other statistics.
  • Transaction data related to in-app purchases.

For ordinary use of ByeBites, weight, height, last-meal time, fullness, fasting sessions, meal preferences, and related health or lifestyle entries you enter are stored locally on your device. AuroZeon does not operate a separate ByeBites cloud account backend that stores those logs for us.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our application.
  • Process and manage your in-app purchases.
  • Generate personalized meal plans based on your preferences and health data.
  • Respond to your inquiries and customer service requests.
  • Send you technical notices, updates, and administrative messages.

Disclosure of Your Information

We may disclose personal information that we collect or you provide:

  • To comply with any court order, law, or legal process.
  • To enforce our rights arising from any contracts entered into between you and us.
  • If we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of our company, our users, or others.

In-App Purchases

Our application offers in-app purchases for premium features. When you make an in-app purchase, you may need to provide payment information to the app store platform (Apple App Store or Google Play Store). This information is collected and processed by the respective platform, not by us. Please refer to the privacy policy of your app store platform for more information.
