Geospatial Services

Spatial data that moves from ingest to decision. workflows, maps, and operational tools for environmental and earth-science problems.

Positioning

For teams that need geospatial systems they can run. not slideware. We design and build pipelines, map experiences, and applied AI that respect data sources, latency, and operational constraints.

Workflow automation

Python and shell-based geospatial pipelines, step logging, and reusable process graphs for ingest, transform, and analysis.

Map-backed products

Interactive maps, geocoding, and location context wired into web and mobile apps. risk views, distance, and place search.

Earth & hazard data

Integration with public geospatial feeds (for example federal wildfire perimeter data) and weather or EO-adjacent APIs where the use case requires it.

Applied research to product

Experience operating research pipelines on shared infrastructure, then packaging usable tools for residents, operators, and partners.

Proof points

  • Wildfire Assistant . NIFC perimeter maps, address risk context, and preparedness guidance in a shipped mobile app.
  • AuroWeaver . visual workflow tooling for Python, Shell, and agent steps used in scientific and environmental scenarios.
  • Community lineage with open geospatial workflow practice (Geoweaver ecosystem and ESIP-adjacent automation patterns).

Who we serve

Research labs, public-interest projects, and organizations that need reliable geospatial software. not a generic IT shop that “also does maps.”

How to engage

Start with a scoped discovery: data sources, update cadence, map UX, and who must act on the output. Then we propose a build or integration plan.

Keep Science Honest

Independent claim records for people who are not domain experts. before capital, a grant, or a public results story.

Positioning

A scientific claim should not become “true” just because it made it into a paper, proposal, or pitch deck. Keep Science Honest starts with the written claim, tests what would prove or challenge it, and seals a record investors, funders, boards, and PIs can show and recheck.

Not peer review. Journals still decide publication. KSH asks whether written claims hold before you invest or communicate results.

Claim-first evaluation

Register exact claims, define evidence that would support or challenge them, and document limitations in plain language.

Sealed deliverables

Confidential sealed packs under SOW: registered claims, evaluation outcomes, and an audit bundle for diligence partners.

Independence

Built for stakeholders outside the research team. investors, sponsors, boards, and auditors who need claim strength without being domain experts.

Recheckable integrity

Recipients can verify a shared audit bundle was not quietly rewritten (ksh verify-report and related checks).

Who this work serves

  • Investors and stakeholders . see which claims behind a project hold before capital or reputation is on the story.
  • Sponsors and funders . capability and limits on the same page, with artifacts you can recheck.
  • Principal investigators . confidential checkpoint while the paper or grant can still change.
  • Auditors . confirm a shared bundle was not rewritten.

How an engagement works

  1. You send claims, scope, and model access or files.
  2. We evaluate strengths, gaps, and examination passes.
  3. You receive a sealed record and audit bundle.

Paid work does not require installing our tools. Sample scientific audit reports and verification procedures are available on the Keep Science Honest site.
