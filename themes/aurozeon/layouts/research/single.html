Geospatial Services

Workflows, maps, and tools that turn spatial data into something teams can use for environmental and earth science problems.

What we do

We design and build geospatial pipelines, map experiences, and related AI features with clear data sources, update needs, and operational limits in mind.

Workflow automation

Python and shell based geospatial pipelines, step logging, and reusable process graphs for ingest, transform, and analysis.

Map backed products

Interactive maps, geocoding, and location context in web and mobile apps, including risk views, distance, and place search.

Earth and hazard data

Integration with public geospatial feeds such as federal wildfire perimeter data, plus weather or related APIs when the use case needs them.

From research to product

We take pipeline and map work from research settings into usable tools for residents, operators, and partner organizations.

Examples

  • Wildfire Assistant: NIFC perimeter maps, address risk context, and preparedness guidance in a mobile app.
  • AuroWeaver: visual workflow tooling for Python, Shell, and agent steps in scientific and environmental work.
  • Open geospatial workflow practice connected to the Geoweaver community and ESIP related automation patterns.

Who this is for

Research labs, public interest projects, and organizations that need reliable geospatial software for a defined operational need.

Next step

Share your data sources, update cadence, map needs, and who must act on the output. We will propose a scoped build or integration plan.

Keep Science Honest

Independent review of written scientific claims for investors, funders, boards, and research teams, before capital or public announcements.

What it is

Keep Science Honest starts with the written claim, tests what would support or challenge it, and produces a sealed record that stakeholders can show and recheck.

This is not journal peer review. Journals decide publication. Keep Science Honest asks whether written claims hold for a funding, investment, or communication decision.

Claim review

Register exact claims, define evidence that would support or challenge them, and document limitations in plain language.

Sealed deliverables

Under SOW: registered claims, evaluation outcomes, and an audit bundle for diligence partners.

Independent audience

Built for people outside the research team: investors, sponsors, boards, and auditors who need claim strength without being domain experts.

Bundle checks

Recipients can confirm a shared audit bundle was not quietly rewritten (ksh verify-report and related checks).

Who uses it

  • Investors and stakeholders: check which claims behind a project hold before capital or reputation is on the line.
  • Sponsors and funders: see capability and limits together, with artifacts you can recheck.
  • Principal investigators: a confidential checkpoint while a paper or grant can still change.
  • Auditors: confirm a shared bundle was not rewritten.

How an engagement works

  1. You send claims, scope, and model access or files.
  2. We evaluate strengths, gaps, and examination passes.
  3. You receive a sealed record and audit bundle.

Paid work does not require installing our tools. Sample reports and verification steps are on the Keep Science Honest site.

Next step

Use the Keep Science Honest site for product details, or contact AuroZeon for a partnership discussion.
