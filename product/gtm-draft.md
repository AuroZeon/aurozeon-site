# Research & Services . GTM draft (site rewrite)

## Positioning

**AuroZeon** delivers three clear offers. not a vague AI brand:

1. **Geospatial Services** . earth-data pipelines and map-backed operational tools
2. **Mobile Development** . iOS/Android products that ship to stores
3. **Keep Science Honest** . independent claim verification before capital, grants, or public results stories

One-liner: *Evidence over hype. Operational software over slideware.*

## ICP (by sector)

| Sector | Primary ICP | Job to be done |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Geospatial | Labs, civic/env programs, applied research teams | Turn spatial data into runnable workflows and usable map products |
| Mobile | Founders / program leads shipping consumer or civic apps | End-to-end mobile product + store delivery + privacy posture |
| Keep Science Honest | Investors, funders, boards, PIs | Know whether written claims hold before acting |

## Value props (honest)

- Geospatial: Wildfire Assistant (NIFC maps), AuroWeaver workflows, Geoweaver-adjacent practice. **do not claim** unshipped EO platforms.
- Mobile: ByeBites, SHOULEMA, XinBridge, Wildfire Assistant live/shipping; Plant GDP upcoming. **do not claim** every app is on both stores.
- KSH: Sealed claim records; **not peer review**; product site keepsciencehonest.com.

## Channels

- Site: `/research/` hub + three sector pages; home `#research` cards
- External: Keep Science Honest domain for claim-check intake
- Contact: support@aurozeon.org / contact flows on aurozeon.com

## Success metrics (suggested)

- Sector page CTR from home research cards
- Contact form inquiries tagged by sector (geo / mobile / KSH)
- KSH site referral traffic from aurozeon.com
- Mobile: store listing updates when Plant GDP releases

## Messaging guardrails

- No silent-success / SBIR Phase I narrative on public research pages (retired as primary story).
- Do not equate KSH with journal peer review.
- Link products as proof points only when they exist on the site.
