# GTM draft: AuroZeon

## Company positioning (fixed)

**Headline:** Advancing AI for a Better Tomorrow

**Support:** Building intelligent solutions that empower businesses and individuals to thrive in a rapidly evolving digital landscape.

**Rule:** Do not replace the company brand with slogan stacks such as "Geospatial. Mobile. Honest claims." or "evidence before hype." Those sound broad and empty. Keep one company theme; put specialty pages under Research.

## What we actually sell / ship

| Offer | Audience | Proof on site |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI powered consumer and civic apps | End users, program partners | ByeBites, SHOULEMA, XinBridge, Wildfire Assistant, Plant GDP (upcoming) |
| Geospatial workflows and map tools | Labs, environmental / civic projects | Wildfire Assistant maps, AuroWeaver workflows |
| Keep Science Honest claim review | Investors, funders, boards, PIs | keepsciencehonest.com |

## ICP

1. Organizations that need a defined AI software product (especially mobile).
2. Teams that need geospatial or environmental decision tools with clear data sources.
3. Stakeholders who need written scientific claims checked before funding or public communication.

## Channels

- Primary: aurozeon.com homepage (company theme) and Solutions product pages
- Secondary: `/research/` topic pages (supporting, not brand)
- External: keepsciencehonest.com for KSH intake

## Success metrics

- Contact inquiries with a clear project type (product, geospatial, KSH)
- Product page engagement (store clicks where live)
- KSH site referrals from aurozeon.com

## Guardrails

- Never overclaim unreleased store listings.
- Keep Science Honest is not journal peer review.
- No personal names in company homepage marketing unless the product page requires team credit.
- No empty slogans: every line should name a capability, product, or next step.
